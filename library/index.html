How to read the books
  • Colour: category. Motif on spine and cover: subcategory.
  • Spine width: number of records, five bands (under 30 · 30–1k · 1k–30k · 30k–1M · 1M or more).
  • Cover art: the dataset's own sample, real positions, records per month or column types. A sample is not coverage.
  • Bookmark: documented reuses. Patina: overdue against its own declared rhythm; static datasets never age.
  • Standing out, with a paper tab: ready but rarely used, one per group. Usage figures: .
  • Order: alphabetical within each group. Position never means rank.
