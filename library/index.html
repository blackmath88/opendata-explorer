How to read the books
  • Colour: category. Motif on spine and cover: subcategory.
  • Spine width: number of records, five bands (under 30 · 30–1k · 1k–30k · 30k–1M · 1M or more).
  • Cover art: the dataset's own sample. Maps: real positions on the canton outline, every map at the same scale. Time series: records per month, with years. Tables: their own column names and types. A sample is not coverage.
  • Bookmark: documented reuses. Patina: overdue against its own declared rhythm; static datasets never age.
  • Standing out, with a paper tab: ready but rarely used, one per group. Usage figures: .
  • Order: alphabetical within each group. Position never means rank. On a question shelf, "The plan" follows the order of the plan's roles.
  • Question shelves: a dashed, empty slot is a role no dataset fills; a plain paper slot stands for more matches than fit, which the list names.
